What does eDPI mean in Apex Legends?

DPI describes your mouse sensor response to physical movement, while in-game sensitivity controls how the game interprets that input. eDPI is calculated by multiplying mouse DPI by in-game sensitivity.

For example, 800 DPI multiplied by 1.00 sensitivity equals 800 eDPI. This is useful for comparing settings within one game, but does not guarantee identical feel across different games.

Find a comfortable setting

There is no single ideal sensitivity for every player. Choose a value you can control comfortably, keep your mouse DPI consistent and make small adjustments. Give yourself time to practice with a new setting.
