What are DPI and eDPI in Valorant?

DPI describes how sensitive your mouse sensor is to physical movement. In-game sensitivity is the value used by Valorant to scale that input. eDPI combines the two values by multiplying mouse DPI by in-game sensitivity.

For example, a player using 800 DPI and 0.35 in-game sensitivity has an eDPI of 280. eDPI is useful for comparing setups within the same game, but it should not be treated as a universal measure across different games.

Choosing a Valorant sensitivity

There is no single best sensitivity for every player. A lower value may make small adjustments easier for some players, while a higher value may suit others who prefer faster turns or have limited desk space. Start with a comfortable setting, practice consistently, and adjust gradually rather than changing it after every match.

When comparing settings, keep your mouse DPI consistent and change one variable at a time. Use the converter to get a starting value, then refine it based on control and comfort in-game.

What is eDPI in Valorant?

No. Aimora calculates a value for you; you enter that value manually in Valorant's settings.
