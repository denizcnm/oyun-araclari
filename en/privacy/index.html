Privacy policy

We value your privacy. This page explains how the current version of the Aimora web tool handles information.

Accounts and personal information

Aimora does not require an account or ask you to submit personal details such as your name or phone number. Game settings entered into the calculator are processed in your browser to produce results.

Local storage

If you use “Save settings”, your selected games and sensitivity values are stored in this browser's local storage on your device so you can load them later. They are not sent to an Aimora account. You can remove them by clearing your browser data.

Cookies and third-party services

The current version is not described as using Aimora-owned analytics or advertising cookies. Fonts may be loaded from Google Fonts; your browser may connect to Google servers for this purpose, subject to Google's own privacy practices.

Changes

This policy may be updated as features are added. Material changes will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the current channel listed on the Contact page.
