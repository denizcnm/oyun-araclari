What are DPI and eDPI?

DPI describes your mouse's sensitivity setting. eDPI is calculated by multiplying mouse DPI by in-game sensitivity. For example, 800 DPI × 0.5 sensitivity = 400 eDPI.

eDPI is most useful when comparing settings within the same game and under comparable conditions. It is not a universal cross-game measurement, because games can use different camera, input and aiming systems.

How to find a comfortable sensitivity

There is no single best sensitivity for every player. Your mouse, desk space, grip, preferred play style and consistency all matter. Start with a value you can control, practice with it, and make small changes only when you have a clear reason.

Keep mouse DPI consistent while testing. When a game has separate ADS, scope or zoom multipliers, review those settings independently instead of assuming a hip-fire conversion covers every situation.

How do I calculate eDPI?
