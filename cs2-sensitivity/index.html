Understanding CS2 eDPI

eDPI is a convenient way to compare mouse settings within the same game. It is calculated by multiplying your mouse DPI by your in-game sensitivity. For example, 800 DPI multiplied by 1.00 CS2 sensitivity gives an eDPI of 800.

eDPI is not a universal cross-game measurement. When comparing CS2 with Valorant or another title, use a game-to-game sensitivity converter rather than assuming that the same eDPI produces the same result everywhere.

How to choose a comfortable CS2 sensitivity

There is no single sensitivity that works best for every Counter-Strike 2 player. Your mouse, available desk space, preferred grip and aiming habits all influence what feels comfortable. Choose a setting you can control consistently and give yourself time to practice before making another adjustment.

If you are testing a new sensitivity, change one setting at a time. Keep your mouse DPI consistent while comparing values, and use the Aimora converter to establish a starting point when moving between supported games.

CS2 sensitivity FAQ

How do I convert CS2 sensitivity to Valorant?

Open Aimora's converter, choose CS2 as the source game and Valorant as the target game, then enter your current CS2 sensitivity and mouse DPI. Test the calculated result in-game and adjust to your preference.

How do I calculate CS2 eDPI?

Multiply your mouse DPI by your CS2 in-game sensitivity. For example, 800 DPI × 1.00 sensitivity = 800 eDPI.

Does Aimora change my CS2 settings automatically?

No. Aimora calculates a suggested value in your browser. You must enter the value yourself in the game's settings.

Is lower sensitivity always better in CS2?

No. Lower and higher sensitivities both have trade-offs. The best choice is one you can control comfortably and use consistently.
