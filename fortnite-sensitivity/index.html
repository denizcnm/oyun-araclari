How Fortnite mouse sensitivity works

Fortnite separates several aiming controls, including look sensitivity and targeting or scoped sensitivity. A general mouse conversion is most useful as a starting point for comparable camera movement; it does not automatically reproduce every building, editing, ADS or scope behavior. Check your in-game X and Y values and keep your chosen ratio consistent while testing.

  3. Enter your current in-game sensitivity and mouse DPI.
  4. Test the suggested value in a practice area or a low-pressure match.

Conversions are estimates for a useful starting point. Field of view, aspect ratio, input settings and game-specific aiming systems can affect the final feel.

What are DPI and eDPI?

DPI describes your mouse's sensitivity setting. eDPI is calculated by multiplying mouse DPI by in-game sensitivity. For example, 800 DPI × 0.5 sensitivity = 400 eDPI.

eDPI is most useful when comparing settings within the same game and under comparable conditions. It is not a universal cross-game measurement, because games can use different camera, input and aiming systems.

How to find a comfortable sensitivity

There is no single best sensitivity for every player. Your mouse, desk space, grip, preferred play style and consistency all matter. Start with a value you can control, practice with it, and make small changes only when you have a clear reason.

Keep mouse DPI consistent while testing. When a game has separate ADS, scope or zoom multipliers, review those settings independently instead of assuming a hip-fire conversion covers every situation.

Fortnite Sensitivity FAQ

Can I convert Valorant sensitivity to Fortnite?

Yes. Select Valorant as the source and Fortnite as the target in Aimora, enter your current value and DPI, then test the result. Treat it as a baseline because Fortnite has additional look and aiming options.

Should Fortnite X and Y sensitivity match?

Many players begin with matching X and Y values for consistent horizontal and vertical movement, but the preferred setup is personal. Change one value at a time and test it.

Does eDPI describe every Fortnite setting?

No. eDPI is DPI multiplied by a selected in-game sensitivity value. Fortnite has separate aiming controls, so one eDPI number cannot represent every mode.
